A little wordplay. Better with friends.

WordPadi turns seven letters into a friendly battle of clever words and smart moves. Challenge a padi or take on Ayo, your computer opponent, and see how much you can make from the tiles in your rack.

Build connected words on a 15 × 15 board, find rewarding letter and word bonuses, and make room for the unexpected. Use a blank to complete a tricky word, swap tiles when you need a fresh start, or earn a bonus by playing all seven tiles in one turn. Every move opens up new possibilities for the next player.

MADE FOR YOUR PHONE
Your tiles stay within reach at the bottom of the screen. Zoom into the board, arrange your letters, and see word checks and a score preview before you tap Submit. You can recall your tiles and try another arrangement before committing your move.

PLAY WITH YOUR PADIS
Start a friendly match and share its invitation code. Scores and turns update as you play, so you can focus on finding your next word.

KEEP YOUR GAMES TOGETHER
Jump between your current game with Ayo and your active friend matches from My games. See the scores, check whose turn it is, and pick up where you left off.

BUILD A FRIENDLY RIVALRY
Visit your head-to-head record with a friend to see wins, losses, draws and recent final scores. Every completed game adds another chapter to your wordplay rivalry.

Whether you have a clever opening or one last tile to play, there is always another word to discover.

Find your word. Challenge your padi. Make every tile count.
